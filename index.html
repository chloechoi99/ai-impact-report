Organisational Context
These tools were built and deployed within the Planning & Analytics Management & PMO Office of a major cloud product division at one of the world's largest enterprise software companies — Fortune 500, 100,000+ employees globally. The function oversees strategic planning, portfolio management, and cross-functional coordination across the division, serving 100+ stakeholders spanning project management, executive reporting, and communications workstreams. The organisation spans multiple sub-departments with dedicated Chief of Staff offices — each responsible for their own planning and operations cycles. All 12 tools in this report were designed, built, and maintained by one person, and several were later independently adopted by peer departments across the broader function.
